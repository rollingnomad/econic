2) It then uses these values to calculate the Floristic Quality Index (FQI) for the community to determine if the community is significant/rare based on the composition of the species present.  FQI is calculated by summing the CC of an inventory of plants and dividing by the total number of plant taxa (n), yielding the mean coefficient of conservatism (Mean CC = Sum of CC /n). The Mean CC is then multiplied by the square root of the total number of plants (n) to yield the FQI (FQI = Mean CC √n).
